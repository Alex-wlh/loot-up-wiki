## What is the safest way to plan Loot Up stats?

Treat stat allocation as a damage-versus-survival decision, not as a solved official ranking. One directly related video recommends putting early World 1 points into Strength because early damage matters, while a second guide warns not to completely ignore Health and Shield later. Neither source establishes a universal best distribution.

The same guide says each level gives three points for Attack, Shield, or Health. This page uses the video terminology as reported; test your own in-game screen because systems can change with updates.

## What do creator guides suggest for early progress?

One creator suggests an early Strength focus through the first world, then starting to add more defensive investment around tougher enemies such as Ice Giants and White Wolves. The speaker gives a sample split of two points in Strength, one in Shield, and one in Health once survival becomes more important.

That is a player’s progression recommendation, not a requirement. If your current equipment or enemies make you fragile, shifting earlier toward defense may be more practical than copying a ratio exactly.

## Can you change your stat build later?

The collected stats video says resets are free every 24 hours and can also be purchased for 29 Robux. Because this comes from a creator video, confirm the current reset rules inside Loot Up before planning around it. A reset option, if still available, is a reason to test a build rather than permanently overcommitting.

## How do stats relate to skills and gear?

Stats are only one part of progression. The game overview also describes gear upgrades and combat abilities, so a build that feels weak may need an equipment or skill adjustment rather than only more stat changes. Read [Skills](/builds/best-skills), [Enchantments](/builds/best-enchantments), and [Armor](/gear/best-armor) as separate systems.
