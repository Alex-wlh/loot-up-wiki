## What is confirmed about Loot Up skills?

The Loot Up Fandom home page includes a Skills section, and Bloxed’s game overview says combat abilities can be unlocked and upgraded with scarce Ancient Skill Scrolls. A direct skills video shows a skills area with a roll action, but its transcript does not provide a complete verified list of effects or cooldowns.

That is enough to explain the system at a high level, but not enough to publish an official-looking tier list.

## Which skill combinations do creators recommend?

In a beginner-to-pro guide, the creator calls **Cinder Slam** and **Thunder Step** one of the best early-game combinations. The same creator calls **Black Hole** a strong skill and describes following it with **Radiant Judgment** as one of the best combos in the game.

These are player-video recommendations. They may be useful combinations to test if you own the skills, but the page does not claim they are the only viable options or that they remain best after every patch.

## How should you use the rolling information?

The skills video shows the player opening a skills area and using a roll. It also describes moving from Spinning Slash toward Spinning Dash, but the automatic transcript is not a reliable substitute for an in-game skill database. Use it as navigation context only; confirm names, availability, and effects in your own game session.

## A safe skills-planning approach

1. Learn how to access the skills and roll interface.
2. Avoid calling any skill “best” solely because a video named it.
3. Test a creator-suggested pairing in the content you currently play.
4. Review [Stats advice](/builds/best-stats) and [Enchantments](/builds/best-enchantments) before changing several systems at once.
