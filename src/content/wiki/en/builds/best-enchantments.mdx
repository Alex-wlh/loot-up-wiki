## How do you enchant weapons in Loot Up?

The official Roblox listing confirms that players can enchant weapons. Bloxed’s Loot Up overview names **Enchant Stones** and **True Enchant Stones** as the materials used for weapon enchanting, and the Worlds page lists Enchantments among World 1 content.

That is the full verified answer from the current source set. It does **not** show the current menu, button order, stone cost, success rate, or whether a weapon can be re-enchanted. For those details, open the live game and treat its current interface as the source of truth.

## Which enchantment facts are verified?

The collected sources support only these system-level facts:

- Enchantments are a World 1 content category.
- Weapon enchanting exists in Loot Up.
- Enchant Stones and True Enchant Stones are named enchanting materials.

These facts are enough to identify the system and its material vocabulary. They are not enough to publish a complete effect list or a click-by-click guide.

## Is there a best Loot Up enchantment?

Not from the evidence collected for this wiki. Creator videos discuss possible effects and personal choices, but the current material set has no second source or in-game verification for an effect table, proc rate, damage multiplier, or universal ranking. This page therefore does not label any enchantment as the best.

If you are deciding whether to spend a stone, first check that weapon in the live game, then compare the result with your current progression needs. See [Weapons Guide](/gear/weapons-guide) for equipment context and [Loot Up Tips](/guides/loot-up-tips) for early-progression priorities.
