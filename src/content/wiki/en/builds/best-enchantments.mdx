## What is verified about Loot Up enchantments?

The official Roblox listing says players can enchant weapons. Bloxed’s game overview adds the material names **Enchant Stones** and **True Enchant Stones**, while the Worlds page lists Enchantments among World 1 content. These sources support the system’s existence and basic material vocabulary, not a complete effect table.

## Which enchantments do creator guides mention?

One guide video says weapons can be enchanted for effects such as fire blasts, ice storms, lightning, or healing. Another creator calls **Storm** one of the best overall enchants, says they use it for general farming and dungeons, and uses **Radioactive** for stronger enemies and boss fights. The same video says **Culling** instantly kills enemies under 15% health.

Those statements are creator reports. They are useful for testing a purpose-based choice, but not proof that one enchant is always strongest.

## How should a player choose an enchant?

Start from the activity you are trying to improve. A general-farming recommendation and a boss-fight recommendation can be different, so do not force one enchant into every situation. Invest scarce materials carefully, especially before you have confirmed the current effects in the live game.

For equipment context, see [Weapons Guide](/gear/weapons-guide) and [Armor Guide](/gear/best-armor). For broader early progression, read [Loot Up Tips](/guides/loot-up-tips).

## What is not known well enough to rank?

The sources do not provide a complete current effect list, probability table, or cross-verified damage comparison. This page therefore does not publish S/A/B ranks, exact proc rates, or a guaranteed “best enchantment.” Update it only when those facts have direct evidence.
