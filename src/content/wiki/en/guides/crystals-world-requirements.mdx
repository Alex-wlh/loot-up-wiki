## Which Loot Up worlds require Crystals?

The Loot Up Fandom Worlds table records Crystal thresholds alongside Level and Gold requirements for Worlds 2 through 5. These are useful planning targets, but they are a source snapshot and should be rechecked after major updates.

| World | Level requirement | Gold requirement | Crystal requirement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| World 2 | 80 | 18k | 20k |
| World 3 | 150 | 44k | 50k |
| World 4 | 225 | 110k | 90k |
| World 5 | 450 | 250k | 200k |

## How should you use this table?

Use the next world’s requirements as a planning checkpoint, not as proof of a fastest Crystal farm. If you are close to a Level or Gold gate, spending resources elsewhere may delay the unlock. If Crystals are the shortfall, keep the target visible and verify the live requirement before changing your route.

The [Beginner Tips](/guides/loot-up-tips) page has general early-world advice, while this page deliberately stays factual about the requirements.

## Were Crystals ever included in codes?

Pro Game Guides recorded `3MIL` and `5MIL` as historical codes that each included 500 Crystals, alongside Coins, EXP, and other materials. That is evidence of past code rewards only. It does not prove that either code remains redeemable or that codes are a dependable current Crystal source.

For a dated current-code snapshot, see [Loot Up Codes](/codes/loot-up-codes). Never plan a world unlock around a third-party code until the live Roblox experience accepts it.

## What is not verified here?

The official Roblox listing confirms the broad loot loop but does not publish Crystal acquisition, shop usage, or World 6 data. The available video transcript also does not provide reliable Crystal mechanics. This page therefore does not claim a best farm, a merchant purpose, or a World 6 requirement.
