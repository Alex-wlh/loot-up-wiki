## What are the safest early Loot Up priorities?

The best-supported early priority is to finish the tutorial and clear the first world before optimizing enchants or a specialist build. A beginner source specifically recommends equipping loot with the highest raw stats early, while the official listing confirms the broader loop of combat, loot, gear upgrades, and weapon enchants.

This approach avoids spending a rare material based on a ranking that may not fit your current drops. If a new item is a clear upgrade, use it; if it is only a sidegrade, keep your resources until you understand the system better.

## How should you approach world progression?

The Fandom Worlds page says each new world introduces stronger enemies, rarer weapons, and better rewards. Treat that as a progression signal rather than a reason to rush past early systems. World 1 lists Forging, the Black Market, Skills, and Enchantments, so it is a useful place to learn what each menu does.

Before trying to unlock another world, check the factual [Crystal world requirements](/guides/crystals-world-requirements) table. It lists the sourced Level, Gold, and Crystal requirements for Worlds 2 through 5; it does not invent a farming route.

## Which later tips are player advice?

Guide-video creators recommend farming the strongest enemies you can defeat efficiently, paying attention to daily quests, and joining the official community or a clan for a smoother experience. These may be useful habits, but they are creator advice rather than official rules or measured optimal routes.

The same distinction matters for enchants: a video can suggest investing in a main weapon, but it does not prove a universal best enchant. Read [Enchantments](/builds/best-enchantments) before deciding how much of your material stock to spend.

## A practical first-session checklist

1. Finish the tutorial and practice dodge timing and ability cooldowns.
2. Replace clearly weaker equipment with better raw-stat loot.
3. Read the [reported Codes page](/codes/loot-up-codes), then redeem only codes that still work in-game.
4. Explore the World 1 systems before spending rare upgrade materials.
5. Use daily quests and efficient enemy farming as optional player-tested habits, not mandatory rules.
