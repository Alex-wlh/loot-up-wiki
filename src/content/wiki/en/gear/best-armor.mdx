## What is confirmed about armor in Loot Up?

Armor is a listed category on the Loot Up Fandom home page, and the official Roblox listing confirms that players upgrade gear. Bloxed’s game overview adds that Forgeguards strengthen armor specifically against boss-tier damage. These are useful system facts, but they do not identify a best set for every player.

## How should you approach armor progression?

Use armor as part of the broader gear-upgrade loop. A creator guide says stronger armor sets often come from each world’s final bosses, which is a helpful progression lead rather than a verified drop table. Compare the equipment you actually have and avoid treating a video recommendation as a permanent current ranking.

The [Loot Up Tips](/guides/loot-up-tips) page explains why early players can prioritize clear raw-stat upgrades before chasing a specialist build.

## What do the video sources mention?

A Loot Up update video mentions a **Jade armor set** in the Chest of Worthiness and reports that the creator needed about 2,500 rings for a full set. Another long-form guide tells players to aim for an **Abyssal Ronin armor set** in World 6. Both are source leads, not confirmation of a complete current World 6 armor list.

The available Fandom Worlds table only provides sourced requirements through World 5, so this guide does not transform these video mentions into a World 6 equipment database.

## What this page does not rank

There is no cross-verified table of armor names, attributes, drop locations, or current balance. This page deliberately does not label Jade, Abyssal Ronin, or any other set as the official best armor. A separate World 6 Armor page remains paused until better evidence exists.
