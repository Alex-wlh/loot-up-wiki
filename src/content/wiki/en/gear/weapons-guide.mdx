## How do you get weapons in Loot Up?

A directly related creator video describes getting more weapons through loot: when a weapon drops, the player can obtain it. The official Roblox listing confirms the broad gameplay loop of slaying enemies and collecting loot. Together, these sources support a loot-based weapon guide, not a named-weapon database.

## What should you do with a new weapon drop?

Compare it against your currently equipped weapon and use a clear upgrade. Early guidance from the collected beginner source recommends equipping drops with higher raw stats before overthinking an optimized build. This is an early-progression rule of thumb, not a substitute for a full current stat comparison.

## How does enchanting fit into weapons?

The official listing says players can enchant weapons, while Bloxed identifies Enchant Stones and True Enchant Stones as related materials. Read [Loot Up Enchantments](/builds/best-enchantments) before committing rare materials: its creator recommendations are clearly labeled, and it avoids claiming a universal best effect.

## Why is there no Best Weapon ranking?

The sources do not provide reliable current weapon names, base attributes, drop locations, or a tested comparison table. Calling a weapon “best” without those facts would be an invented ranking. This page is therefore a practical weapons guide; a future ranking needs a documented test method and current data.
